Immediate Dentures

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the immediate-denture lectures by Lect. Dr. Mustafa Saadi Ali (PDF pages 564–580).

  • Definition, indications (philosophical patient) and contraindications
  • Advantages and disadvantages; conventional (CID, relined) versus interim (IID, replaced by a second denture)

  • Diagnosis, impressions (single full-arch custom tray versus sectional tray), try-in, cast trimming and the surgical guide
  • Insertion, the first 24 hours (do not remove), the 24-hour adjustment, and follow-up lining and recall
