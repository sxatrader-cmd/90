Post Crowns, Impression Materials, and the Final Impression

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 8–9 (post crowns) and Lectures 9–10 (impressions) (PDF pages 55–77).

  • Post-crown types, Peeso reamers, two-thirds root length or crown length (whichever is greater), a 3–5 mm apical seal, and the 1×4 mm keyway
  • Impression-material classes: agar (71–100 °C / 30–50 °C), alginate (pour within 15 minutes), polysulfide, condensation silicone, polyether and addition silicone
  • Special tray (2–3 mm, 5 mm cervical, made at least 9 hours ahead) and gingival retraction (10-minute cord, Expasyl, Nd-YAG)
  • Single-mix, double-mix and putty-wash techniques, post-canal reinforcement, disinfection, and CAD-CAM digital impressions
  • Total run time: 56 minutes.
