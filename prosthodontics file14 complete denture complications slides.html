Complications in Complete Denture Wearers

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the 5th-year lecture on complications in complete-denture wearers (PDF pages 426–455): planned recall, long-term tissue and occlusal change, dry mouth, hard and soft linings, the flabby ridge, fracture and tooth debonding, gagging, burning mouth syndrome, and speech disturbance.

  • Yearly first recall, then every 2–3 years; resorption (~1 mm/year, lower anterior four times the upper) and the fit–occlusion vicious cycle
  • Hakan et al. 2012 frequencies; saliva functions, the mirror test, and IPH before a new denture
  • Chairside hard relines (~1-year life, butylmethacrylate), short-term and long-term soft linings and their indications
  • Flabby (combination) ridge: surgical controversy and mucostatic versus mucodisplacive impressions
  • Midline fatigue fracture (29%) and debonded teeth (33%); gagging including under-extension; BMS types 1–3; and the four speech relationships
  • Total run time: 44 minutes.
