Deep-Seated Caries, Vital Pulp Therapy, and Fluoride-Releasing Materials

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 11 by Dr. Ahmed H. Ali (PDF pages 212–219) on vital pulp therapy, and a lecture on fluoride-releasing materials (pages 220–227).

  • Indirect pulp capping when the lesion is three-quarters of the dentine on a radiograph, with a stepwise re-entry at 3–6 months
  • Direct capping for a 24-hour uninflamed exposure (about 80% success); a Cvek pulpotomy of 1–2 mm (about 95% after trauma)
  • Calcium hydroxide’s tunnel defects versus a stable MTA bridge, a 4–6 week wait, and recall from 3–4 weeks
  • GIC, RMGI, compomers (3 mm or 2 mm increments, 40-second cure, fluoride for at least 300 days) and giomers with fluoride recharge
  • Total run time: 26 minutes.
