Pontics, Connectors, and the Final Impression (Conventional and Digital)

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 6 and 7 by Dr. Lamis A. Al-Taee (PDF pages 168–195).

  • Pontic designs: a saddle that should never be used, a 20% occlusal-width reduction, ovate and modified-ovate (1–1.5 mm), and a hygienic pontic with a 3 mm space and 3 mm thickness
  • Siebert’s ridge classes, Spear’s 2.5 mm immediate ovate, Alvelac (resorbed in 2–6 months), and connector position (key mesial of the pontic, keyway distal of the anterior abutment)
  • Elastomers: a 4 mm polysulfide spacer, polyether in less than 5 minutes, PVS poured after 1–2 hours, and spaced versus unspaced putty-wash
  • CAD/CAM (direct versus indirect, open versus closed) and disinfection: rinse after the chemical, and spray polyether and hydrophilic PVS rather than soaking them
  • Total run time: 54 minutes.
