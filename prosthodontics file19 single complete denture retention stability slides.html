Single Complete Denture, Retention and Stability

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 1–3 by Asst. Lect. Ghasaq Hisham (PDF pages 503–526): the single complete denture and combination syndrome, how complete-denture retention is obtained, and the factors of stability and support.

  • Single complete-denture combinations, Yurkstas / Swenson / Bruce modification, and Kelly’s combination syndrome
  • Fracture precautions, porcelain versus acrylic versus gold or amalgam occlusals, and the poor prognosis of a mandibular single denture
  • Retention: anatomical factors, adhesion (24 cm² / 14 cm²), cohesion, atmospheric pressure, House PPS sizes in this lecture, adhesives and why suction chambers are avoided
  • Stability, the neutral zone, and a U-shaped vault as the ideal palatal form
  • Support: mandibular buccal shelf and maxillary hard palate as primary areas; ridge crests as secondary; implants and mini-implants
  • Total run time: 24 minutes.
