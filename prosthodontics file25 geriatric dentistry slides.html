Geriatric Dentistry

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the geriatric-dentistry lectures by Lect. Dr. Mustafa Saadi Ali (PDF pages 608–619).

  • Definitions of aging, genetic and environmental factors, and young / mid-old / oldest-old age groups
  • How geriatric practice differs (86% have at least one chronic disorder; polypharmacy; dementia)
  • Related systemic diseases, and age changes in saliva (25–30% acinar loss; xerostomia in about one fifth), mucosa, teeth, tongue and periodontium
  • Sharry classes (TOLBUDS, TOLAD, TOLN) and psychological disorders
  • Treatment options, tooth- versus implant-supported overdentures, and denture care with Tell–Show–Do
  • Total run time: 20 minutes.
