Attachments in Overdentures

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the 5th-year lecture on attachments used with overdentures (PDF pages 481–490): functions and selection, rigid versus non-rigid types, stud, bar, magnet and O-ring systems, and hygiene and follow-up care.

  • Functions of an attachment and the twelve factors that guide selection
  • Rigid versus non-rigid behaviour, and when a short dowel or unfavourable tooth distribution indicates a non-rigid attachment
  • Stud systems: Zest (intraradicular), Ceka and Dalbo
  • Bar units versus bar joints: Hader, Ackerman/C.M., and Dolder (including the Schubiger unit)
  • Cobalt–samarium magnets, O-rings, denture and abutment hygiene (0.2% chlorhexidine; 0.025% fluoride), and prosthodontic follow-up
  • Total run time: 20 minutes.
