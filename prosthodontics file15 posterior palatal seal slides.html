Posterior Palatal Seal

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the 5th-year lecture on the posterior palatal seal of the maxillary complete denture (PDF pages 456–467): anatomy and functions, vibrating lines, House classification, recording methods, errors, and when to record the seal.

  • PPS definition and functions, including compensation for PMMA shrinkage toward the bulky ridge
  • Post-palatal and pterygomaxillary seals, foveae as a guideline only, and the mid-palatal raphe
  • Anterior versus posterior vibrating lines, and House Classes I–III (widths 3–4 mm, 2–3 mm, or a single bead)
  • Conventional carving (1–1.5 mm / 0.5–1 mm), fluid wax (ZOE or plaster, head flexed 30°), and arbitrary scraping
  • Under-extension as the commonest poor-seal cause; over-extension, under- and over-postdamming; adding a PPS to an existing denture; and recording before versus after try-in
  • Total run time: 28 minutes.
