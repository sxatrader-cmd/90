Indirect Aesthetic Inlays, Direct Composites, and Dental Lasers

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 13 by Dr. Abdullah Al-Shammaa (PDF pages 228–242) on indirect aesthetic inlays and direct composites, and lectures by Dr. Ahmed Hamid Ali (pages 243–255) on dental lasers.

  • Indirect inlays and onlays so that only a thin lute shrinks, with 70–80% tooth reinforcement and a repairable composite option
  • Composite from Bowen in 1962: filler amounts, types by particle size, shrinkage of 1.67–5.68%, and increments of 2 mm or a 4–5 mm bulk fill
  • Laser types including Er:YAG at 2.94 µm with a pulp rise kept below 5.5 °C, and argon curing in 8 seconds versus 44 seconds
  • PIPS with 17% EDTA at 50 microseconds and 20 mJ, power- and energy-density formulae, and green, amber or clear safety glasses
  • Total run time: 40 minutes.
