Residual Ridge Resorption (RRR)

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 4–5 by Asst. Lect. Ghasaq Hisham (PDF pages 527–543) on residual ridge resorption: healing and Atwood orders, direction and rate, causes, and prevention.

  • RRR is chronic, progressive, irreversible and cumulative; sockets fill with bone in about 6 months but remodeling continues for life
  • Atwood orders I–VI and ACP mandibular height Types I–IV (21 mm or more down to 10 mm or less)
  • Centripetal maxillary versus centrifugal mandibular resorption; the anterior mandible resorbs about four times faster (Woelfel 4.2 vs 2.3 sq in)
  • Anatomic, metabolic (hormones, diet, Type I/II osteoporosis) and mechanical causes, including Wolff’s law and bruxism
  • Prevention: snow-shoe coverage, overdentures (0.6 mm vs 5 mm bone loss in a canine-supported study), and implants
  • Total run time: 24 minutes.
