Cements, Liners and Bases

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 8–9 by Dr. Linz (PDF pages 52–70): pulp-protection cements, liners and bases, and the clinical depth classification of cavities.

  • Why liners and bases are used, and how a thin liner differs from a thermal-insulating base
  • Zinc phosphate: 40% phosphoric acid, a 2–8 minute set, mixing in 60–90 seconds on a 21 °C slab, and a 10-minute wait before trimming
  • ZOE (pH 7, about 8 minutes) must not be used under composite; polycarboxylate bonds chemically to calcium
  • Glass ionomer (Types I–III), resin cements, varnish (two thin coats), calcium hydroxide (pH 9.2–11.7), and shallow-to-exposure cavity protocols
  • Total run time: 40 minutes.
