Radiography, Working Length, Cleaning and Shaping, and Irrigation

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 5–7 by Prof. Dr. Hussain Al-Huwaizi (PDF pages 48–71).

  • Applications of radiography, apex anatomy, a stable reference point, and working-length methods including the lecture’s 3 mm add/subtract examples
  • Electronic apex locators (electrical resistance) and the consequences of a length that is too long or too short
  • Schilder’s mechanical and biological objectives, eleven instrumentation principles, and manual techniques: Ingle standardized, step-back, step-down, balanced force, and crown-down
  • Ideal irrigant, smear layer, eight modifying factors, saline, NaOCl (including the NaOCl accident), EDTA 17% for one minute, and chlorhexidine 0.2% vs 2%
  • Syringe irrigation (needle size and 2 mm from the apex), ultrasonic and sonic activation, and laser-activated irrigation (Er:YAG, PIPS, SWEEPS)
  • Total run time: 1 hour 2 minutes.
