Emergencies, Restoring Endodontically Treated Teeth, Endo–Perio Lesions, and Bleaching

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 9–12 by Prof. Dr. Anas Falih Mahdi (PDF pages 135–152; page 140 is almost empty).

  • Carrotte’s emergency classification, and management of pulpitis, abscess (do not leave the tooth open; do not inject into the swelling), cracked tooth, flare-up and the hypochlorite accident
  • Why dentin is weaker after endodontics (9% moisture loss), onlays and endocrowns, posts that retain but do not strengthen, and a 2–3 mm ferrule (or 5 mm of apical gutta-percha)
  • Five types of endodontic–periodontal lesion, the pulpal-versus-periodontal table, palatal grooves, hemisection and root resection
  • Causes of discoloration including tetracycline and fluorosis, Superoxol, sodium perborate and 10% carbamide peroxide
  • Thermocatalytic and walking-bleach techniques (2 mm glass-ionomer barrier) and adverse effects, including cervical resorption above 30% hydrogen peroxide
  • Total run time: 52 minutes.
