Copy Denture

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the copy-denture lectures by Lect. Dr. Mustafa Saadi Ali (PDF pages 620–632).

  • A copy (duplicate, template, replica) denture transfers contours for neuromuscular control and allows limited modifications
  • Especially useful for older patients, stained bases, a spare denture, or experimental occlusion; contraindicated if the old denture has serious defects
  • Three clinical visits only; temporary copies can be modified gradually then copied to a definitive denture
  • Four techniques: duplicating flask, soap container (110°F, 15–30 psi, 30 minutes), silicone putty, and agar-agar
  • Problems: box rigidity, alginate distortion of the ridge, careful reline and jaw-relation records, and careful stock-tooth placement
  • Total run time: 18 minutes.
