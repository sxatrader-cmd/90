Resin Composites, Light-Curing, Bonding, and Class III–V Technique

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 12–14 by Dr. Linz (PDF pages 109–132): resin-based composites, light-activation sources, etching and bonding generations, and direct Class III, IV and V restorations.

  • Composite since 1962: conventional 75–80% filler at 8 µm, microfilled 35–50% at 0.01–0.04 µm, hybrids 75–85% at 0.4–1 µm
  • QTH light around 470 nm, an argon laser at 488 nm, PAC and LED sources, and a higher C-factor in Class I and V
  • Enamel etch 15–30 seconds (about 30 MPa); dentine 10–15 seconds or 15 seconds only; total-etch generations 4–5 and self-etch 6–7
  • Class III–V designs: a 0.25–0.5 mm bevel at 45°, a 1–2 mm Class IV bevel, and curing the bond 10 seconds and each increment 20 seconds
  • Total run time: 44 minutes.
