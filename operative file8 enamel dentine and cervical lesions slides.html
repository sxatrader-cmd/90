Biologic Considerations of Enamel and Dentine, and Cervical Lesions

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 3–5 by Dr. Abdullah Al-Shammaa (PDF pages 148–172): enamel and dentine biology in operative dentistry, then carious and non-carious cervical lesions and dentinal sensitivity.

  • Enamel HV = 275 (4–5 times dentine), 30,000–40,000 rods/mm², a 10- to 20-fold etch, and shade before isolation
  • Tubules 15,000–20,000/mm² at the DEJ to 45,000–65,000/mm² at the pulp, and inner dentine about eight times more permeable
  • Tensile strength 10 versus 98 MPa, a third of the fracture resistance after endodontic access, and hydrodynamic sealing of tubules
  • Root caries judged by softness, NCCLs at 5–85%, a 6-week wait after gingival surgery, and an open or closed sandwich
  • Total run time: 42 minutes.
