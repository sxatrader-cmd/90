Neutral Zone in Complete Dentures

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the neutral-zone lectures by Lect. Dr. Mustafa Saadi Ali (PDF pages 633–645), the last block of the syllabus.

  • The neutral zone is where outward tongue forces are neutralized by inward lip and cheek forces; locate the potential denture space
  • Indicated especially for a severely atrophic lower ridge and poor neuromuscular control; advantages include stability and tongue space
  • Buccinator, modiolus and tongue must be respected; centralization or lingualization can violate the zone
  • Recording at impression, jaw-relation, try-in or finished-denture stages, using a plaster index to set the teeth
  • Compared with a conventional lower denture: anteriors and premolars slightly lingual, posteriors buccal; larger tissue surface, smaller volume
  • Total run time: 20 minutes.
