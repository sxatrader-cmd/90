Pulp Irritants, Inflammatory Conditions, and Restorative Effects on Pulpal Health

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 9–10 by Dr. Ahmed H. Ali (PDF pages 173–193): pulp irritants and inflammatory conditions, then how operative procedures, liners and restorations affect pulpal health.

  • Pulp is 75% water; remaining dentine under 300 µm makes acid etching hazardous; a 5.5 °C rise for 10 seconds can damage the pulp
  • Reactionary versus reparative dentine (about 15 days), infected versus affected dentine, and a pulp-polyp pulpotomy only if there is no necrosis
  • Ultra-speed 300,000 rpm needs a water coolant; a 1–2 mm base is a thermal barrier; keep liners off the margins
  • Unreacted resin monomer 1.5–5%, freshly mixed amalgam more cytotoxic than set amalgam, and the accumulative insult to the pulp
  • Total run time: 36 minutes.
