Access Opening (Part II), Instruments, and the Rubber Dam

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 3–4 by Prof. Dr. Hussain Al-Huwaizi (PDF pages 24–47).

  • Four pre-access guidelines, access burs including the Endo Z bur, and step-by-step anterior and premolar openings
  • Six pulp-cavity anomalies, and access errors (perforation, cutting more apically, a narrow or over-extended opening, and a labial or proximal entrance)
  • General and intracanal instruments: barbed broach, K-reamer and K-file, K-flex, Hedström, spreader and plugger
  • ISO standardization (D0, 2% taper, D16, lengths 21/26/31 mm) and the lecture’s colour-coding sequence
  • Four modes of action, and the rubber dam: reasons, sheet and frames, Ivory clamps, and the three application methods
  • Total run time: 50 minutes.
