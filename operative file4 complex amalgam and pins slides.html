Complex Amalgam Restorations, Cusp Capping and Pins

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 11 by Dr. Linz (PDF pages 71–83): complex posterior amalgam restorations, cusp capping, slots and pins, the Thread Mate System, and why amalgam restorations fail.

  • When a complex amalgam is indicated, and cusp capping once the occlusal opening exceeds two thirds of the intercuspal distance (at least 2 mm of amalgam)
  • Secondary retention: horizontal coves, vertical locks, and slots 0.5 mm pulpal to the DEJ and at least 0.5 mm deep
  • Self-threading, cemented and friction-locked pins; TMS sizes Regular, Minim, Minikin and Minuta; a 2 mm depth-stop drill at 300–500 rpm
  • Five places a pin-retained restoration can fail, how to manage a broken, loose or perforating pin, and why amalgams fail
  • Total run time: 34 minutes.
