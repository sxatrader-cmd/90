Indirect Tooth-Coloured Restorations, Ceramic Veneers, and CAD/CAM

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 18–22 by Dr. Abdullah Al-Shammaa (PDF pages 256–279) on indirect tooth-coloured inlays and onlays, ceramic veneers and inlay-onlay preparation, and CAD/CAM techniques. This is the last Operative equivalency file.

  • Inlays versus onlays, first- and second-generation indirect composites, and when ceramic is not indicated in parafunction
  • Semi-direct 60-second cures, a flexible model seated in 8 minutes, and heat treatment for 18 minutes at 100 °C
  • Veneer depths of 0.3–0.7 mm and 1.0–1.5 mm, a 2 mm ceramic occlusal thickness, and 2 mm versus 3 mm cusp-coverage rules
  • CAD/CAM from Duret in 1983 and CEREC in 1987, STL design, 3- to 5-axis milling, and additive printing with nearly 100% material use
  • Total run time: 44 minutes.
