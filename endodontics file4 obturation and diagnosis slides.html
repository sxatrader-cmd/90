University of Baghdad · College of Dentistry · Endodontics · 4th year Lecture 8 and Prof. Anas Lecture 1

Obturation of the Root Canal, and Endodontic Diagnosis

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 8 by Prof. Dr. Hussain Al-Huwaizi and Lecture 1 (diagnosis) by Prof. Dr. Anas Falih Mahdi (PDF pages 72–90).

  • Objective of obturation (ingress and egress), four aims, and the criteria for filling, including the controversy about a negative culture
  • Gutta-percha composition (β-form percentages), disinfection, standardized versus non-standardized cones, silver points, and sealer functions
  • Underfilling versus overfilling (Ng et al. 2007, 2 mm), lateral condensation with tug-back, and warm vertical compaction
  • Five stages of diagnosis, pain history, extra-oral and intra-oral examination, and tracing a sinus tract with a #25 or #30 cone
  • Palpation, percussion, mobility, pulp tests (cold, heat not more than 5 seconds, EPT, cavity test), the bite test, staining, and the cementoma radiographic warning
  • Total run time: 50 minutes.
