Cavity Liners and Cement Bases, including MTA, Biodentine and TheraCal LC

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 8 by Dr. Rasha (PDF pages 84–108): liners and bases in this lecture’s wording, glass-ionomer Types I–V, and the calcium-silicate cements MTA, Biodentine and TheraCal LC.

  • A typical cement base of 1–2 mm, and a calcium-hydroxide liner when the floor is judged to be within 0.5 mm of the pulp
  • This lecture’s glass-ionomer Types III (lining), IV (fissure sealants) and V (orthodontic cementation), which differ from the Linz grouping in File 3
  • MTA: grey versus white, a set pH of 12.5, 2 hours and 45 minutes (and later about 3–4 hours), carried with an amalgam carrier or the MAP system
  • Biodentine in about 12 minutes, and TheraCal LC as a light-cured one-paste liner with a short-term high calcium release compared with Dycal
  • Total run time: 44 minutes.
