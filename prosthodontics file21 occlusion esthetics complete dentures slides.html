Occlusion in Complete Dentures, and Esthetics

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 6–9 by Asst. Lect. Ghasaq Hisham (PDF pages 544–563) on complete-denture occlusion and esthetics.

  • Occlusion versus articulation; CR (bone-to-bone) should coincide with CO (tooth-to-tooth) in complete dentures
  • Six lecture requirements (including incisal clearance and mesiodistal unlocking) and four objectives (tissues, mastication, stability/retention/support, phonetics and esthetics)
  • Balanced, lingualized and monoplane schemes; Hanau’s quint; Theilman’s formula as written in the lecture: (C × I) ÷ (OP × CC × CA)
  • Condylar guidance ~33° (fixed); incisal guidance 10°, not exceeding 20° (dentist-controlled); the dentist mainly changes the compensating curve and cusp inclination
  • Esthetics: impression and VDO, size/shade/mold guides, SPA, a natural arrangement, characterization, and Frush and Fisher’s dentogenic concept
  • Total run time: 40 minutes.
