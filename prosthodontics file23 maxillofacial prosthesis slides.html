Maxillofacial Prosthesis

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the maxillofacial-prosthesis lectures by Lect. Dr. Mustafa Saadi Ali (PDF pages 581–591).

  • Definitions, acquired / congenital / developmental defects, indications and objectives, and the maxillofacial team
  • Extraoral prostheses (nasal, auricular, orbital, cranial, radiation carrier, carrier stent) and intraoral types
  • Obturators: functions, Aramany Classes I–VI, and surgical (~10 days), interim (2 weeks to 3–6 months) and definitive types
  • Feeding appliance, speech aid, palatal lift (incompetence versus anatomic deficiency), and Cantor and Curtis mandibular-resection types
  • Construction steps (monoplane occlusion is usually used) and retentive aids (anatomic, adhesives, magnets, clasps, buttons, clips, implants)
  • Total run time: 22 minutes.
