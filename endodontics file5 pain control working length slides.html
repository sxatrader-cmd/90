Pain Control in Endodontics, and Working-Length Determination

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 2–3 by Prof. Dr. Anas Falih Mahdi (PDF pages 91–104).

  • Aδ versus C fibres (C fibres about three times more numerous), neurogenic inflammation, and factors that lower the pain-reaction threshold
  • Esters and amides, sodium-channel block, vasoconstrictors, infection and pH 5–6, and confirming pulpal anaesthesia with a pulp test
  • IANB (1.8 ml, 15 minutes, 1 hour; two cartridges), Gow-Gates and Akinosi–Vazirani, maxillary infiltration 87–92%, PDL 0.2 ml per root, intraosseous and intrapulpal anaesthesia
  • This lecture’s radiographic working-length method: start 1 mm short, subtract 1 mm for the constriction, or 2 mm if there is external resorption
  • Apex locators: 650 ohms, a pacemaker contraindication, precautions, and generations one to six
  • Total run time: 38 minutes.
