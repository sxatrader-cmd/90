Introduction, Cavity Nomenclature, Hand and Rotary Instruments, and Sterilization

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 1 by Dr. Linz and Lecture 2 by Dr. Rasha (PDF pages 2–29): what operative dentistry is, G.V. Black’s classes, instrument formulas, and sterilization numbers.

  • Operative dentistry treats defects that do not need full coverage; caries is an irreversible microbial disease of calcified tooth tissue
  • G.V. Black’s Classes I–VI, and the enamel and dentine triangles of occlusal versus smooth-surface spread
  • Black’s three- and four-number formulas, a 1–2 mm contra-angle balance, low speed 500–15,000 rpm and high speed above 160,000 rpm with water coolant
  • Autoclave 250 °F / 15 min / 15 psi (or 273 °F / 3 min / 20 psi), chemiclave, dry heat above 320 °F for 1–2 hours, 2–3% glutaraldehyde for 6–10 hours, and a 30-second handpiece flush
  • Total run time: 44 minutes.
