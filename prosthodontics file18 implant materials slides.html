Characteristics of Ideal Materials for Dental Implants

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the 5th-year lecture on ideal dental-implant materials (PDF pages 491–502): definitions and classifications, polymers and ceramics, titanium and surface modification, coatings, guided bone regeneration, and bone-graft mechanisms.

  • Four conditions for implant success, osseointegration, and Strunt’s versus Combe’s classifications
  • Polymers and PEEK, vitreous carbon, alumina and zirconia, and bioactive HA and bioglass coatings
  • Titanium properties (4.51 g/cm³, 110 GPa, 1668°C), CPT grades 1, 2 and 4, and the adverse interface reaction reported for Ti-6Al-4V
  • Machined, blasted, etched, sintered and plasma-sprayed surfaces, ceramic-coating methods, and bone-to-implant contact
  • GBR materials including e-PTFE and TEFGEN-FD, and osteogenesis versus osteoinduction versus osteoconduction (autograft, allograft, alloplast)
  • Total run time: 28 minutes.
