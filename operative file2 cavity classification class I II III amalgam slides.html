Cavity Classification, and Class I, II and III Amalgam Preparations

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 4–6 by Dr. Linz (PDF pages 31–51): how cavities are classified, G.V. Black’s seven steps, and the Class I, II and III amalgam preparations.

  • Simple, compound and complex cavities, Sites 1–3, and Black’s seven steps from outline to the toilet of the cavity
  • Class I and II: a facio-lingual width of no more than ¼ ICD, a 90°–110° cavosurface, and occlusal amalgam 1.5–2.0 mm thick
  • Class II: a 2 mm marginal ridge, a No. 245 bur, an isthmus no wider than ¼ ICD, and 0.5 mm clearance with the adjacent tooth
  • Class III amalgam: usually the distal of a canine; initial axial depth 0.5 mm inside the DEJ (0.75 mm on the root); a No. 14 bur cuts a 0.25 mm gingival groove
  • Total run time: 38 minutes.
