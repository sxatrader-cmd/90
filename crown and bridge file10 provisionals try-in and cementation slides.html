Provisional Restorations, Clinical Try-In, and Cementation

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the two Lecture 10 blocks by Prof. Dr. Adel F. Ibraheem (PDF pages 220–248): provisional restorations, then clinical try-in, adjustment and cementation.

  • Provisional requirements, PMMA shrinkage of about 8%, bis-acryl, a 4 mm post-crown wire, CAD/CAM blanks with about 1% residual monomer, and ZOE as the usual temporary cement
  • Try-in without anaesthesia, a pressure-free die check, 0.05 mm shim stock, and a two-direction probe test for short, long and open margins
  • Luting: film ≤25 µm and at least 70 MPa; zinc phosphate 14,000–16,000 PSI (pH about 3.5); silicophosphate 22,000; polycarboxylate 1,300 / 480 PSI; glass ionomer 18,600 PSI
  • The 350 MPa bonding rule, RMGI delayed expansion, 5% HF for 20 seconds plus silane for 60 seconds, 10-MDP for zirconia, and a 1-hour wait after cementation
  • Total run time: 58 minutes.
