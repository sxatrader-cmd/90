ACP Classification, and Arranging Teeth in Abnormal Jaw Relations

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of the 5th-year lectures on the American College of Prosthodontists classification of complete edentulism (Classes I–IV and the four diagnostic criteria) and on arranging anterior and posterior teeth when the residual-ridge relationship is Class II or Class III (PDF pages 401–423).

  • ACP complete-edentulism Classes I–IV, benefits of a classification system, and the four diagnostic criteria
  • Mandibular bone-height Types I–IV, maxillary ridge Types A–D, mandibular muscle attachments A–E, and MMR Classes I–III
  • The “any more-complex criterion raises the whole class” rule, surgical and systemic modifiers, and when to refer
  • Normal ridge relationship (80° posterior crest line), centripetal / centrifugal resorption, and skeletal percentages (55.5% / 10% / 34.5%)
  • Class II and Class III anterior and posterior tooth arrangement, including first-premolar elimination and cross-arch tooth interchange
  • Total run time: 32 minutes.
