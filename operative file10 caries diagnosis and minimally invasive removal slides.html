Caries Diagnosis, Activity, Prevention and Minimally Invasive Removal

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 8 by Assist. Prof. Dr. Ahmed H. Ali (PDF pages 194–211): how to detect and diagnose caries, how to tell an active lesion from an arrested one, prevention and treatment, and minimally invasive removal technologies.

  • Dry clean teeth for more than 5 seconds; magnification can improve detection by 50%; a sharp explorer can cavitate a white-spot lesion
  • DIAGNOdent at 655 nm scores 0–99; bite-wings are best for proximal surfaces but cannot prove cavitation
  • Fluoride varnish 5% NaF (22,600 ppm F), sealants that can arrest sealed dentine lesions, and ICON for the inner half of enamel or the outer third of dentine
  • Air abrasion, Carisolv, Er:YAG, a self-limiting Smartprep bur, and ozone for 20–40 seconds (75–100% harder at one year)
  • Total run time: 24 minutes.
