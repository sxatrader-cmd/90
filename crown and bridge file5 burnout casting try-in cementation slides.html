Burnout and Casting, Cleaning and Finishing, Clinical Try-In, and Permanent Cementation

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 16–18 by Dr. Alaa (PDF pages 101–118).

  • Burnout at 200 °C for 30 minutes then 480–650 °C for 45 minutes, and the bluish reducing zone as the only flame that must touch the metal
  • Precious, semi-precious and non-precious alloys, centrifugal casting (three or four turns), and why PFM alloys get no flux (1200 / 1300 / 1500 °F)
  • Quenching, pickling in 50% hydrochloric acid, try-in without anaesthesia, floss and two-direction probe tests, and a 1 mm burnished margin
  • Zinc phosphate (14,000–16,000 PSI, pH about 3.5), polycarboxylate, glass ionomer, resin and RMGI, and a 3–4 minute seating bite on a wooden stick
  • Total run time: 38 minutes.
