Endodontic Radiography and Intracanal Instruments

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lectures 4–6 by Prof. Dr. Anas Falih Mahdi (PDF pages 105–121).

  • Bisecting versus paralleling techniques, practical film tips, and the SLOB (buccal-object) rule
  • Rapid processing at 100 °F, digital systems (CCD, storage phosphor with a 90% dose cut, and indirect scanning), and CBCT (small FOV, 60–75 μm voxels)
  • Six groups of intracanal instruments, K-type versus H-type, Golden Mediums, and Gates-Glidden versus Peeso
  • File taper, rake angle and pitch, then ProFile, ProTaper, RaCe, Twisted File, Wave One and ProTaper Next with this lecture’s numbers
  • The self-adjusting file, Giromatic/Endo-Eze, and sonic versus ultrasonic devices (cavitation and acoustic streaming)
  • Total run time: 48 minutes.
