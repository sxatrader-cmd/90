Adhesive Bridges, Treatment Planning, Dental Ceramics, and Failure

English slides with a detailed, very simple Iraqi Arabic explanation of Lecture 12 (resin-bonded bridges), diagnosis and treatment planning, Lecture 14 (dental ceramic) and Lecture 13 (failure) by Prof. Dr. Adel F. Ibraheem (PDF pages 249–275).

  • Adhesive bridges: Rochette perforations, Virginia meshes, Maryland HF etching, and Panavia on sand-blasted metal — plus when no prosthesis is needed
  • Failure: caries as the commonest cause, pulpal pain up to six months, one loose retainer on a fixed-fixed bridge, and Ante’s law called redundant here
  • Ceramic strengthening: potassium ions 35% larger, Hi-Ceram 40% and In-Ceram 85% alumina, and transformation-toughened zirconia (“ceramic steel”)
  • Feldspathic porcelain (kaolin 11–12%, feldspar 70–90%), vacuum firing, and fusing ranges including under 850 °C for titanium
  • Total run time: 52 minutes.
